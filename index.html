Scored Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Introduction

Scored is a scoreboard and game companion application. This Privacy Policy explains how Scored collects, uses, processes and shares information when you use the application.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Scored may process the following information:

  • Player names entered into the application.
  • Scores, game results and match history.
  • Card images submitted through the Snap Pic scanner.
  • Technical information required to operate the application.
  • Advertising-related information processed by Google AdMob.

3. Camera and AI Card Scanner

Scored includes an optional camera-based feature called Snap Pic. Camera permission is required when using this feature.

When a user takes a card photo using Snap Pic, the image is sent securely to our AI processing server. The server analyses the image to recognise cards and calculate scores.

Images are processed to provide the requested result and discarded after processing. Our AI server does not retain submitted images or request logs.

Card images are used only for the requested card recognition and scoring function. They are not used for advertising.

4. Game Information and Match History

Scored allows users to enter player names, scores, game results and other game-related information.

This information is used to provide scoreboards, custom games, game history and related features.

Game information may be stored locally on the user's device. Its retention depends on the app's storage implementation and user actions, such as clearing app data or uninstalling the application.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

Scored uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process information to provide, measure and improve advertisements.

Depending on the user's device settings, region and advertising configuration, this information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers and device information.
  • Approximate location information.
  • Ad impressions and interactions.
  • App activity and diagnostic information.

Advertising information is handled according to Google's privacy policies and the applicable advertising settings.

For more information, visit: Google Privacy Policy .

6. In-App Purchases

If in-app purchases are made available in Scored, payments will be handled through Google Play Billing.

Payment information is processed by Google Play. Scored does not directly collect or store users' payment card details.

7. Data Sharing

Scored may transmit information to service providers when necessary to provide its features.

  • Our AI processing server receives card images for card recognition and score calculation.
  • Google AdMob processes advertising-related information to provide advertisements.
  • Google Play handles application distribution and any applicable in-app purchases.

We do not sell users' personal information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through Scored.

Connections to our AI processing server are intended to use secure HTTPS communication.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Card images submitted through Snap Pic are processed and discarded. Our AI server does not retain submitted images or request logs.

Game information and match history may remain stored according to the app's storage settings and user actions.

Advertising-related information may be retained by Google and its advertising partners according to their own privacy policies and retention practices.

10. Children's Privacy

Scored is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing its features.

If you believe personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us using the email address below.

11. Your Privacy Choices

Users can choose whether to use optional features such as the camera-based card scanner.

Camera permission can be managed through Android device settings.

Users can also contact us with questions or requests concerning information processed through Scored.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Scored's features, services or data practices change.

Updated versions will be published on this webpage. The latest update date will be shown at the top of the policy.
